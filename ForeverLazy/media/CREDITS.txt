Questie and the libraries
-------------------------
Quest, NPC, object, item and drop data, and the group message format, come from Questie (GPL-3.0): https://github.com/Questie/Questie
Forever Lazy is built on HereBeDragons, AceComm, AceSerializer, CallbackHandler, LibDeflate and LibStub, which keep their own licences (see their folders under libs).
Flight path information comes from the wowforever.be flight path guide: https://wowforever.be/guides/flight-paths/
Forever Lazy is not affiliated with Blizzard Entertainment or the Questie team.
