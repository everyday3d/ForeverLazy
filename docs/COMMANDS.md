# Commands

Back to the [README](../README.md) · [Feature guide](FEATURES.md)

`/lazy` and `/forever` do the same thing. Most of these are also a click on the minimap button's menu.

## Everyday

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/lazy now` | Lazy Mode: point at the best next thing |
| `/lazy go <place>` `go stop` | Plan and follow a whole trip |
| `/lazy plan [all]` | Plan the most efficient quest route (`all` includes other zones) |
| `/lazy window` | The plan window |
| `/lazy skip` `skip clear` | List or bring back quests you skipped |
| `/lazy check` | Is everything loaded and working |
| `/lazy tour` `export` `import` `new` `welcome` | Tour, share settings, what's new, welcome window |

## Finding things

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/lazy inn` `repair` `flight` `bank` `ah` `mailbox` `stable` `spirit` `battlemaster` | The nearest one (add a number for the next) |
| `/lazy trainer` `/lazy cooking` | Class trainer, or a profession trainer |
| `/lazy boat <place>` `boat mark` `boat list` `boat stop` | Boat and zeppelin routes |
| `/lazy hearth` `hearth set` `fp` `fp list` | Hearthstone and flight points |
| `/lazy herb <name>` `ore <name>` `gather` `fish` | Gathering routes and fishing spots |
| `/lazy journey` `generate` `regenerate` `go <n>` | Your zone-by-zone route to 60 |
| `/lazy way <x> <y> [name]` `here` `list` `next` `remove <n>` `clear` | Waypoints |

## Quests and crafting

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/lazy class` `dungeons` `bag` `nextzone` | Class quests, dungeons, sleeping bag chain, next zone (each takes `go`) |
| `/lazy chain` `farm` | Quest chain follow-up, best place to farm |
| `/lazy craft [profession] [goal]` `craft go` `craft bank` | Crafting plan, materials, bank |
| `/lazy books` `zone` `turnin` `list` `done` `share` `say` | Library book collection |

## Group

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/lazy follow [name]` `follow off` | Follow a group member |
| `/lazy party` `shareplan` `acceptplan` `duo` | Group features |
| `/lazy net on` `off` | Share boat docks with your guild and group (hidden messages) |

## Settings and display

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/lazy preset minimal` `leveler` `duo` `full` | Switch modules in one go |
| `/lazy lock` `unlock` `scale <n>` `reset` | Move, resize and reset the arrows |
| `/lazy own` `compass` `pet` | My quests arrow, compass bar, pet reminder |
| `/lazy flighttimes` | Flight times learned so far |
| `/lazy macros` `macros remove` | Make or remove the addon's macros |
| `/lazy toggle` `both` `quest` `friend` `off` | RestedXP friend arrows |
| `/lazy why` | Explain where the plan's arrow comes from (for bug reports) |
| `/lazy guide` `bigger` `smaller` `tv` `normal` | The levelling guide window and its text size (couch and TV) |
| `/lazy xpmap` | XP hot spots on the world map, on or off |
| `/lazy pace` `pace quests` | Your measured XP per hour, the time to your next level, and your fastest and slowest quests |
| `/lazy drops <item>` | The mobs you have seen drop an item, with the rate |
| `/lazy session` | This session: XP, quests, kills and gold |
| `/lazy hive` `hive reset` | What the hive has learned, or forget it |
| `/lazy has <item>` | What each of your characters has of an item |
| `/lazy hive on` `off` | Turn the Hive on or off (learning and sharing) |
| `/lazy hive who` `rares` `rare` | Other players per zone, reported rares, and an arrow to the nearest rare |
| `/lazy grind` | The best mobs to grind in this zone near your level, by XP per hour |
| `/lazy meter` `meter off` `meter dps` `meter overall` `meter rows 8` `meter reset` `meter debug` | The damage meter in a chat tab: on, off, what to show, this fight or overall, how many players, clear it, what it sees |
| `/lazy danger` | The places in this zone where the most players have died |
| `/lazy coach` `coach say` | The Coach: the best thing to do now by measured XP per hour (a window, or a few lines in chat) |
| `/lazy nodes` `nodes <name>` `nodes farm [ore|herb]` `nodes pins on|off` `nodes clear` `nodes debug` | Saved ore and herb nodes: what you have, find a node by name (a window), farm them with the arrow, the map pins, forget them, check tracking |
| `/lazy level` `level <zone>` `level steps` | Plan the whole zone for the fastest levelling (a window of what to get, where to kill, what to hand in); with a zone name, go there first |
| `/lazy class quest` `class here` `class list` | Your class quests and what to do for each, what the zone you are in needs, or the plain chat list |
| `/lazy dungeons why <quest>` `dungeons purge` | Why a quest counts as a dungeon quest, and take dungeon quests out of the plan |
| `/lazy plan off` `plan on` | Turn the plan off and keep your place, then pick it up again |
| `/lazy keep` `keep <item>` `keep remove <item>` `keep off` `keep on` | Items a vendor must not keep (the tooltip warns, a sale is bought back) |
| `/lazy pace reset` | Clear your measured XP per hour and start measuring again (drop counts and quest times are kept) |
