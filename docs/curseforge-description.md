# STOP ALT-TABBING TO GUIDES!! PRESS ONE BUTTON AND GO!!!

**Forever Lazy** is a levelling companion for **WoW Forever** that does the THINKING for you!! It knows your quests, your bags, your professions and the MAP, and it points an ARROW at the next thing worth doing. OHHHHH!

No Questie needed. Forever Lazy carries its OWN quest data (and still works with Questie if you have it).

## THE ARROW AND THE PLAN
- **A route planner.** Your quests put in a smart order by XP for the time, nearby quests counted, the little ones left out.
- **The arrow points at the nearest kill or collect spot,** stays in the zone you are in, and moves on when you have cleared it.
- **Turn-ins come first** when you are standing right there.
- **Level a WHOLE ZONE** with `/lazy level`: what to get, from whom, where to kill or collect, what to hand in.
- **Autopilot** for vendor and trainer stops, and the plan follows you across zones, boats, trams and flights.
- **Dungeon help.** Walk into a dungeon and it shows exactly what your quests need in there.
- **Your own quest tracker** (it can REPLACE WoW's): plan order, XP, live objectives, who to turn in to.

## THE QUEST MAP
Skulls where you kill it, diamonds where it drops, a yellow ! for quests to pick up and a yellow ? for hand-ins, with coloured areas so you can SEE where to go. Click a pin or a quest and the arrow goes THERE. Works with OpenMap too!

## ONE BAG TO RULE THEM ALL
- All your bags in ONE window, with a search box, quality borders, item levels, free slots and money.
- **Real sections** (Travel, Quest, Equipment, Food and drink, Potions, Consumables, Trade goods and more) that only show when you carry something for them. The Hearthstone sits at the very top.
- **The bank opens side by side** with your bags so you can move stuff across.
- Look inside the bags, bank and mail of your OTHER characters.
- Takes over the bag keys when Bagnon is not running.

## NAMEPLATES, TOOLTIPS AND MORE
- A **sword** on mobs you need to kill, a **loot bag** on mobs that drop what you need, a gold **!** on mobs that drop a quest starter.
- **Interrupt marker:** a casting mob shows its spell. Green = your interrupt is ready, red = cooldown, grey = you have none.
- **Quest item reminder:** stand where a quest wants an item used and a button with the item pops up.
- **New spell rank?** It takes the old rank's place on your action bars, by itself.
- **Tooltips that talk:** hover a mob, item or NPC and see which quest, how far along you are, and the REAL drop chances.
- **A damage meter** in its own chat tab, using the game's built-in meter, with every move a player used on hover.
- **Tell the party** when you finish a quest. Job's done sounds, couch mode, talent reminders, ore and herb node memory, an item protector so you never sell the sleeping bag, and chat tweaks like Prat (all off until you turn them on).

## INSTALL
Download **`ForeverLazy-x.y.z.zip`** from the project files (NOT the "Source code" download) and put the **`ForeverLazy`** folder in `World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns\`. Then type `/lazy` in game, or click the minimap button.
